# Tutoring Assessment

Language for the tutor-operated reading assessment and its connection to instructional resources.

## Language

**Assessment Run**:
One anonymous administration from tutor confirmation through completion or early stopping.
_Avoid_: Test record, student record

**Checkpoint**:
A broad group of original words that determines whether an instructional skill band needs focused probing.
_Avoid_: UFLI test set, placement-test section

**Concept Probe**:
A five-word focused group used to gather evidence about one lesson-level decoding concept.
_Avoid_: Quiz, worksheet

**Demonstrated Gap**:
A concept for which the student misses at least two words in its Concept Probe.
_Avoid_: Failure, diagnosis

**Lesson Evidence**:
A correct response to a word explicitly mapped to an instructional lesson. A word's decoding Requirements and the lesson it supplies evidence for are distinct: `map` requires Lessons 2, 1, and 6 and supplies placement evidence for Lesson 6, while an early blending word can supply evidence for Lesson 5.
_Avoid_: Mastery proof, diagnosis

**Starting Point**:
The highest instructional lesson supported by Lesson Evidence anywhere in the Assessment Run. Failed higher-band samples locate where to probe but do not themselves imply support. The result is a tutor-facing suggestion rather than a formal placement.
_Avoid_: Official placement, score

**First Observed Gap**:
The earliest Demonstrated Gap at or above the Most Advanced Demonstrated lesson. Together those two signals bracket what was established and where focused difficulty first appeared; lessons between them may be untested.
_Avoid_: First unmastered lesson, diagnosis

**Mastery Frontier**:
The neighborhood between the Most Advanced Demonstrated lesson and the next Concept Probe that the student cannot pass with two correct responses. Successful nearby probes move the frontier and increase the next jump; a failed jump narrows the search back toward the frontier.
_Avoid_: Fixed placement range, distant failed band

**Recommendation**:
A Starting Point or additional Demonstrated Gap paired with links to available official instructional resources.
_Avoid_: Prescription

**Resource Link**:
A direct link to an official UFLI Google Slides deck, Decodable Passage, Home Practice sheet, or Roll and Read activity for a recommended lesson.
_Avoid_: Hosted resource, copied resource

**Tutor Result**:
The gated, printable summary containing Recommendations and every administered word judgment.
_Avoid_: Student feedback, permanent record
