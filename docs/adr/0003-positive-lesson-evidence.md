# ADR 0003: Select the Starting Point from positive lesson evidence

## Status

Accepted

## Decision

Give every assessment word two mappings: the lessons or components required to decode it, and the instructional lesson for which a correct response supplies placement evidence. Choose the highest lesson supported by at least one correct mapped word anywhere in the Assessment Run; failed higher-band samples guide adaptive probing but never create unsupported placement evidence.

This represents non-letter concepts such as Lesson 5 blending without confusing them with letter requirements and prevents a failed broad band from inflating placement. After a distant Checkpoint miss, focused probing resumes immediately above demonstrated evidence; two correct responses advance an accelerating frontier, while two misses narrow it. Tutor Results pair the resulting adjacent demonstrated lesson and gap.
